What changes in 90 days

Here's what clients who do the work typically experience:

Quieter nights

Binges get rarer and smaller. A slip turns into a next-morning reset instead of a three-day spiral.

You see it coming

You can spot your triggers (stress, loneliness, exhaustion, "I was good all day") before you reach for food.

Cravings lose their power

You'll have tools you've practiced dozens of times, so a craving becomes a wave you ride out instead of an order you follow.

New beliefs

"I can't be trusted" becomes "I know how to take care of myself." This is the change that lasts.

Less food noise

Food takes up less of your day, and you feel less guilt after you eat.

A plan that's yours

You leave with a written plan for holidays, hard weeks and stressful nights, so the change sticks after coaching ends.

We measure it. You score yourself on Day 1 and Day 90 so you can see the change in numbers.

Questions

Is this a diet?

No. There's no meal plan and no food rules. We work on why you eat at night, not what you eat.

Will I lose weight?

That isn't what this program promises. We focus on bingeing, cravings and the beliefs behind them. Many women find their body changes when the fight with food ends, but the goal is freedom, not a number on the scale.

What if I slip during the program?

You will, and that's part of it. Each slip shows us something about your triggers, and you learn to recover in hours instead of days.

Is this therapy?

No. This is coaching. If you think you might have an eating disorder, please see a licensed professional too. Coaching can work alongside that care.
