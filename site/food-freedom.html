Questions

Is this a diet?

No. There's no meal plan and no food rules. We work on why you eat at night, not what you eat.

Will I lose weight?

That isn't what this program promises. We focus on bingeing, cravings and the beliefs behind them. Many women find their body changes when the fight with food ends, but the goal is freedom, not a number on the scale.

What if I slip?

You will, and that's part of it. Each slip shows us something about your triggers, and you learn to recover in hours instead of days.

How does coaching work?

Open the calendar in the app and book a 1:1 session with me whenever you want extra support.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime. Your 7-day trial is free, and if you cancel before it ends you won't be charged.

Is this therapy?

No. This is coaching. If you think you might have an eating disorder, please see a licensed professional too. Coaching can work alongside that care.
